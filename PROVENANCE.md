# Provenance

## Project origin

This repository was initialized on 2026-09-04 as an independent research project following discussion of extending standards-coalition theory from binary/formal compatibility to a continuous, endogenous degree of interoperability.

The motivating idea is that standards may permit different degrees of interoperability: some standards are close to technically fixed/common interfaces, while others leave firms scope for differentiation. A continuous interoperability choice may therefore affect consumer switching/mismatch frictions, network benefits, price competition, welfare, and coalition incentives.
