## Decision 1 — novelty unit

The revival's novelty unit is **not** compatibility-induced differentiation, continuous standards policy, coalition formation, product repositioning, or a policy-ranking reversal in isolation.

The only surviving novelty unit is the whole-game theorem class:

`standards coalition -> endogenous bloc depth -> costly post-policy product repositioning -> Bertrand competition -> national welfare -> blocking/stability`,

with a result characterizing absorption versus non-absorption and, in a non-absorption region, a coalition-stability implication that disappears when product positions are fixed.

## Decision 2 — killed claims

The following are permanently killed for the revival branch as headline claims:

- compatibility first induces later product differentiation;
- standardization policy affects product characteristics;
- continuous compatibility/standardization policy is novel;
- standards coalitions trade off network benefits and competition;
- regional versus multilateral standards can differ in stability;
- coalition structure changes later product choices and prices;
- continuous standardization policy changes a downstream strategic margin and reverses an optimal-policy ranking;
- nonzero post-standard repositioning is itself a contribution.

## Decision 4 — binding own prior art

Two own-research projects must be treated as internal prior art rather than contribution material:

1. `private-compatibility-standards-coalitions`;
2. `standardization-scope-direction-innovation` / “Standardization Scope and Endogenous Innovation Portfolios.”

In particular, SSDI already occupies the generic architecture `continuous standardization policy -> endogenous downstream strategic response -> changed optimal policy`. The revival must add a genuinely distinct coalition-stability interaction, not import the SSDI mechanism.

## Decision 5 — Stage 2 whole-game verdict

No exact prior model or immediate theorem was identified that reproduces the residual whole-game result class.

Classification: **POTENTIALLY NOVEL, BUT NARROW AND RESULT-DEPENDENT.**

## Decision 6 — Stage 3 authorization

Stage 3 may search for one minimal economically justified mechanism capable of breaking or qualifying the Stage-1 policy-adjustment absorption result.

Stage 3 may not:

- tune parameters until a desired coalition wins;
- add a primitive solely to force `s_I>0`;
- merge several unrelated mechanisms;
- use generic compatibility-induced differentiation as novelty;
- reuse SSDI's innovation-portfolio mechanism as the revival contribution.

## Decision 7 — required Stage 4 test inherited from Stage 2

Any Stage-3 winner must be sent to Stage 4 with the following fatal contribution test:

> Does the FULL model with endogenous repositioning produce a stable-partition change, blocking reversal, stability-threshold shift, or private/social stability wedge that the otherwise identical fixed-position and exogenous-depth benchmarks cannot reproduce?

If not, the revival must be terminated.
