## Binding novelty decision

The project survives Stage 6 only on a narrow result-level contribution:

> In a government standards-coalition game with endogenous realized-interoperability depth, costly product repositioning after policy choice can shift the bilateral-union versus international-standardization national-welfare blocking threshold relative to the otherwise identical fixed-position policy game, and can therefore change the stable partition in a selection-safe higher-redesign-cost region.

## Permanently killed novelty claims

The project may not claim novelty from:

1. compatibility-induced differentiation;
2. network/interoperability benefit versus competition trade-offs;
3. continuous standards/compatibility policy;
4. standards breadth/depth as a stability margin;
5. coalition membership followed by an endogenous firm/resource action;
6. regional versus multilateral standards stability;
7. costly product repositioning itself;
8. continuous standardization changing downstream strategy;
9. private versus social preferred standards depth/participation;
10. the superseded low-`gamma` selection-free stable-set result.

Menegaki & Serfes's 2026 research program is now mandatory prior art for future manuscript work, especially:

- *Coalition Stability with Complementary Goods*;
- *Breadth, Depth, and Coalition Stability in Complementary Oligopoly*;
- the current working-paper listing *The Architecture of Voluntary Standards in Complementary Oligopoly*.

Their work materially occupies coalition stability, breadth/depth, interoperability/compatibility governance, and private/social depth language. The revival must distinguish itself on the post-policy costly product-repositioning effect on the government standards-coalition blocking threshold.

## Own-prior-art constraint

- `standardization-scope-direction-innovation`, which already covers continuous standardization -> endogenous downstream strategic reallocation -> welfare/policy effects;
- `private-compatibility-standards-coalitions`, which already covers formal government standards partitions -> private response -> national welfare -> coalition stability.
