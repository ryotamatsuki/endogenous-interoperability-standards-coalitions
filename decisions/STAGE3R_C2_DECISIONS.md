# Stage 3 Re-entry — C2 Decision Addendum

Date: 2026-09-04

This addendum records the decisions generated by the C2 re-entry. It is authoritative together with `decisions/DECISION_LOG.md` until the next consolidated decision-log maintenance pass.

## D-029 — Re-enter Stage 3 for C2 as a distinct mechanism

Decision: ACCEPTED / COMPLETED

C2 is evaluated independently after C1's Stage-4 NO-GO. Bilateralization is not treated as a repair to C1.

## D-030 — Reject max/min technology as the C2 contribution

Decision: REJECTED AS CORE CONTRIBUTION

`max{a_i,a_j}` is directly exposed to unilateral/no-veto converter provision; `min{a_i,a_j}` and product analogues are weakest-link/mutual-veto technologies. The veto/consensus mapping from firms' choices into effective compatibility is already explicit in de Palma–Leruth–Regibeau and Garcia–Vergari.

## D-031 — Natural smooth bilateral OR technology passes welfare integrability but fails the mechanism test

Decision: VERIFIED / C2 PREDICTION REJECTED

For

`A_ij=a_i+a_j-a_i a_j`

and B0-style inverse demand,

`partial p_i/partial q_j=partial p_j/partial q_i`,

so a coherent consumer-surplus microfoundation exists.

At symmetric member implementation `a`, with `x=v(2a-a^2)`, the exact marginal operating-profit returns satisfy

`MB_I/MB_U=2/(1+x)`.

On `0<v<=1/4`, this ratio is at least `8/5>1`. The natural smooth bilateral free-riding technology therefore strengthens, rather than weakens, implementation incentives in the larger IS coalition.

## D-032 — Standard public-good free-riding is insufficient

Decision: REJECTED AS SURVIVING C2 MECHANISM

For a standard voluntary public good `pi_i=B(G)-c(e_i)`, `G=sum e_i`, under `B''<=0`, `c''>0`, symmetric aggregate provision rises with coalition size. Lower individual effort is not enough to generate lower effective interoperability in a larger coalition.

Independent pairwise public-good links likewise have regime-invariant per-link implementation unless a common capacity/scope constraint is added.

## D-033 — Stage 3 re-entry verdict for C2

Decision: `NO-GO`

C2 bilateral implementation public-good/free-riding is terminated and is not authorized for Stage 4.

A positive result would require importing another mechanism such as dilution, common engineering capacity/scope costs, or coalition-wide shared interoperability infrastructure.

The project remains at Stage 3 for a distinct mechanism search. A common-interoperability-infrastructure contribution game, if pursued, must be treated as a new candidate rather than relabeled C2.
