- **D-6R2-02 — Old joint-endogeneity headline remains killed.** The claim that endogenous harmonization depth and endogenous product positions are jointly necessary for the reversal is not identified at the canonical witness because Stage 11R's `B-EQ` reproduces FULL with exogenous equilibrium depths.

- **D-6R2-05 — Strongest closest-paper threat.** The strongest reconstruction attack is `Gandal & Shy (2001) + Ruiz (2004)`. Gandal-Shy supplies the standards-union/national-welfare architecture; Ruiz supplies policy -> endogenous characteristics -> prices and excessive differentiation. The audited propositions do not make the C-ESD coalition-ranking reversal an immediate corollary.
- **D-6R2-06 — Ruiz finding.** Ruiz is structurally very close but does not absorb the surviving result: its endogenous-location extension reports qualitatively similar government-policy outcomes rather than a policy-ranking reversal.
- **D-6R2-07 — 2024-2026 frontier finding.** No directly absorbing result was located. Huang et al. (2026) kills novelty for weighted interoperability-network design; Kretschmer et al. (2025) kills novelty for strategic downstream response to mandated interoperability.

- **D-6R2-09 — Assumption-driven-result test.** The pairwise friction map alone does not generate the reversal because B-T uses the same map without the sign change. However, policy-depth endogeneity itself is not identified as necessary; the essential demonstrated channel is post-policy repositioning at the relevant depth.
- **D-6R2-10 — Permitted manuscript novelty language.** Permitted: “Allowing firms to strategically re-differentiate after a standards policy can reverse the national-welfare ranking between international standardization and a regional standards union relative to a fixed-position evaluation of the same standards architecture.”

- **D-6R2-12 — Literature evidence language.** Use: “No directly absorbing result was located in the audited search as of 2026-09-04.” Do not state that no such prior result exists.
