# Stage 6 C-ESD Decisions

Date: 2026-09-04

These decisions are authoritative together with `decisions/DECISION_LOG.md` until the next consolidation pass.

## D-042 — Kill policy-to-product-differentiation setup novelty

Decision: **KILLED**

Ruiz (2004) already has governments choose standard-recognition policy before firms choose product characteristics and later compete in prices. C-ESD may not claim novelty from this timing alone.

## D-043 — Kill partial-compatibility / standards-union stability novelty

Decision: **KILLED**

Gandal–Shy, Matutes–Padilla, Economides–Skrzypacz and Ding–Ko–Shen already establish forms of partial compatibility, standards coalitions/unions, or coalition-proof partial compatibility. The fact that an SU can be stable is not a contribution.

## D-044 — Kill continuous government compatibility-policy novelty

Decision: **KILLED**

Klimenko (2009) directly analyzes continuous government compatibility policy with network externalities and international coordination. Continuous policy depth is an ingredient, not a novelty claim.

## D-045 — Kill broad strategic-response-to-interoperability claim

Decision: **KILLED AS BROAD CLAIM**

Recent interoperability work, including Kretschmer–Rasch–Shekhar–Wenzel (2025), already shows that mandated interoperability can induce strategic firm responses that offset intended regulatory effects. C-ESD may claim only its specific product-repositioning / coalition-stability interaction if that survives.

## D-046 — Preserve the Stage-4 FULL-only interaction result

Decision: **SURVIVES NOVELTY RE-KILL**

Authorized main contribution candidate:

`Delta_M^(B-T)<0`, `Delta_M^(B-X)<0`, but `Delta_M^(FULL)>0`.

Policy endogeneity alone and product-position endogeneity alone each select IS, while their interaction induces SU-member re-differentiation sufficient to reverse national coalition preferences and make the three SUs stable.

No audited prior theorem directly reproduces this result or makes it an immediate corollary.

## D-047 — Reclassify contribution route

Decision: **NEW INTERACTION RESULT / MECHANISM, NOT SETUP NOVELTY**

C-ESD should no longer be presented as a novelty claim based on combining standards coalitions, network effects, continuous compatibility, or endogenous product differentiation.

The publication claim must be proposition-level: endogenous government standard depth × endogenous firm product repositioning generates a coalition-stability reversal unavailable from either nested benchmark.

B0 remains an institutional benchmark, not an algebraically nested model.

## D-048 — Stage 6 verdict

Decision: **GO -> GO TO STAGE 7 WELFARE / GENERALITY**

The surviving result passes the whole-game absorption test, subject to a serious remaining threat: a referee can characterize the architecture as a synthesis of Ruiz (2004) and Gandal–Shy (2001).

Stage 7 must therefore establish economic generality/conditions for the reversal and test dependence on the Salop/redesign specification. Killed claims remain dead.
