## D-045 — Freeze exact national-welfare decomposition

Decision: **ACCEPTED**

`Delta_M = Delta Pi_M + Delta CS/3`.

The coalition preference reversal occurs iff the domestic-profit gain exceeds the member's one-third share of aggregate consumer-surplus loss.

At the canonical witness:

- `Delta CS/3=-0.0325785`;
- `Delta Pi_M=+0.0341498`;

Therefore the Stage-4 reversal is a domestic-rent-capture result, not a consumer-welfare gain.

## D-048 — Generalize the adjustment-cost condition without changing the baseline

Decision: **ACCEPTED AS GENERALITY INTERPRETATION**

The quadratic redesign cost is not a novelty claim. For a general regular convex adjustment cost `C(d)` with `C(0)=C'(0)=0` and positive curvature, SU's nonzero policy-induced marginal operating-profit gradient produces positive member displacement from the inherited positions.

The headline reversal requires an intermediate effective curvature:

- too much curvature -> insufficient repositioning -> `Delta_M<0`;
- intermediate curvature -> rent gain crosses the national-welfare threshold -> `Delta_M>0`;
- too little curvature -> possible global location jumps / ordering changes invalidate the selected local branch.

For `v=0.04,s_bar=0.25`, the upper welfare threshold is `gamma_W=0.132983`, while the audited global-best-response lower transition is around `gamma≈0.10`.
