## Whole-game reconstruction findings

### A. No single-paper absorber located

No audited paper scores `YES` on all eight substantive dimensions. In particular, none combines:

1. country/government regional-versus-international standards coalition;
2. an intensive standards/compatibility margin;
3. network/compatibility value;
4. a **separate** product characteristic chosen by firms after standards policy;
5. price competition;
6. national welfare including domestic producer rents;
7. coalition blocking/stability;
8. a benchmarked IS/SU ranking reversal caused by the post-policy product response.

### B. Strongest composite reconstruction

The most damaging synthesis is:

- **Gandal–Shy (2001)** for the three-country standards-union, network and national-welfare/participation architecture;
- **Ruiz (2004)** for the government-policy → endogenous product-characteristics → price timing and the strategic over-differentiation channel;
- standard network/compatibility IO for the compatibility-versus-competition mechanism.

This composite makes the C-ESD **setup** unsurprising. It does not, however, deliver the surviving result as an immediate corollary: Ruiz explicitly obtains robustness of his qualitative government-policy equilibrium when product characteristics become endogenous, and Gandal–Shy contains no post-policy repositioning equation that signs an IS/SU welfare reversal.

Composite classification: **STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE**, not `NEAR-ABSORBS` under the canonical result-level kill rule.

### C. Strongest post-repair institutional reconstruction

Takarada et al. (2020) is especially close after Stage 4R. It already has three countries, national/regional/multilateral standards, a jointly selected regional common standard, national welfare and core blocking; regional standards alone can be in the core while the world-welfare-maximizing multilateral agreement is blocked.

Thus C-ESD cannot claim novelty for coalition-level harmonization, regional-versus-multilateral standards stability, or the global-versus-national wedge. What remains absent is the post-policy endogenous horizontal repositioning feedback.

### D. Stage-11R benchmark correction

The column `FULL-only reversal` must not be interpreted as evidence that two endogenous margins are jointly necessary in C-ESD. Stage 11R's `B-EQ` fixes depth at the FULL equilibrium value while leaving positions endogenous and reproduces FULL at the canonical witness. Therefore the defensible residual is the `B-T` versus post-policy-repositioning comparison, not a claim that policy endogeneity is necessary.
