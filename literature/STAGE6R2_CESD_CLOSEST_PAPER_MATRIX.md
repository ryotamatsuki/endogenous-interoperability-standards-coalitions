## Whole-game absorption result

No audited paper contains all eight core elements required for direct absorption: government standards regime/coalition, an intensive compatibility margin, network effects, post-policy endogenous differentiation, price competition, national welfare with domestic producer rents, coalition stability, and a benchmark-identified qualitative coalition-ranking reversal.

The strongest two-paper reconstruction is **Gandal–Shy (2001) + Ruiz (2004)**. Gandal–Shy supplies the three-country standards-union / national-welfare / coalition-participation architecture. Ruiz supplies the missing government-policy -> endogenous product-characteristic -> price sequence and the producer-rent versus consumer-surplus mechanism. However, Ruiz's endogenous-location extension explicitly reports that the basic government policy result is qualitatively robust; it does not derive a standards-union participation reversal caused by repositioning. The C-ESD sign reversal therefore is not an immediate quoted proposition from the two papers.

## Important identification caveat

Stage 11R established that the old `B-T<0, B-X0<0, FULL>0` narrative does **not** identify a necessary interaction between policy endogeneity and product-position endogeneity. At the canonical witness, fixing harmonization depth exogenously at the FULL equilibrium value while keeping product locations endogenous (`B-EQ`) reproduces FULL. Thus the defensible residual is narrower: **policy-induced strategic product re-differentiation can overturn the IS/SU ranking relative to fixed product positions.**
