## Directly checked papers and findings

### Gandal & Shy (2001)
Journal page and RePEc working-paper record confirmed: three-country standardization unions, network effects, conversion costs, national welfare and SU-versus-international-recognition incentives. No post-policy endogenous product-location stage was located.

### Ruiz (2004)
Author-uploaded full text was directly inspected. Timing is government standard-recognition policy -> endogenous product characteristics (in the extension) -> prices. Governments maximize domestic consumer surplus plus domestic-firm profits. Ruiz explicitly states that endogenous product characteristics generate excessive differentiation under mutual recognition, but the main government policy result remains qualitatively robust rather than reversing. This is the most important direct evidence against an automatic `Gandal-Shy + Ruiz = C-ESD reversal` argument.

### Klimenko (2009)
Publisher, RePEc and author-institution pages confirmed continuous compatibility policy, network externalities, strategic governments and international agreements. No endogenous post-policy product repositioning / coalition-stability reversal was located in the audited sources.

### Economides & Flyer (1997/98)
Direct working-paper source confirms network externalities, coalition structures, technical standards and the compatibility-versus-horizontal-differentiation trade-off. Coalition choice is by firms, not welfare-maximizing governments, and there is no C-ESD nested-benchmark reversal.

### Economides & Skrzypacz (2003)
SSRN record confirms technical standards coalition formation with degree of compatibility, network effects and oligopoly. Coalition formation is private-firm affiliation, not national policy; no post-policy endogenous product repositioning.

### Baake & Boom (2001)
Publisher/RePEc sources confirm endogenous inherent quality, network externalities, compatibility and price competition. Timing differs importantly: quality is chosen before compatibility/adaptor choice. No national standards coalition stability.

### Jonard & Schenk (2004)
Author-uploaded/full-text record confirms circular product space, network goods, compatibility and price competition. No government coalition / national-welfare stability stage.

### Huang, Tan, Teh & Zhou (2026)
Author webpage and SSRN record checked. Interoperability is a weighted network; the paper characterizes prices and optimal network configurations. The abstract reports industry-wide full interoperability as total-welfare- and platform-optimal. No government coalition formation or endogenous product-location response was located in the audited record.

### Kretschmer, Rasch, Shekhar & Wenzel (2025)
SSRN record checked. Mandated interoperability triggers strategic response in a network market, centered on privacy/data strategy. This is close at the channel level but does not provide national standards coalitions or the C-ESD coalition-ranking reversal.

## 2024-2026 frontier checks

Searches covered mandated interoperability, DMA interoperability, platform interoperability, endogenous differentiation/design responses, partial interoperability, standard-setting coalitions and interoperability networks. The frontier clearly contains strategic responses to mandates and weighted-network design, but no directly absorbing result was located in the audited search that combines policy-induced differentiation with national coalition participation and a fixed-position-versus-repositioning coalition-ranking reversal.

## Newly discovered / strengthened threats

- The strongest threat remains **Ruiz (2004)** for the downstream sequence and welfare mechanism.
- **Economides & Skrzypacz (2003)** is a stronger coalition-formation analogue than a generic compatibility citation because standards coalitions and compatibility degree are endogenous, albeit chosen by firms rather than governments and without post-policy repositioning.
- Recent **Huang et al. (2026)** strengthens the need not to claim novelty for partial/weighted interoperability networks or welfare-optimal interoperability architectures.
- **Kretschmer et al. (2025)** permanently kills any claim that strategic downstream response to mandated interoperability is new.

## False positives / non-absorbing families

- Legal/policy commentary on DMA interoperability without a solved strategic coalition model.
- Empirical interoperability studies without endogenous coalition formation.
- Standards-organization voting / SEP papers without post-policy differentiation and national welfare.
- Compatibility models where differentiation is fixed or chosen before compatibility.

## Inaccessible / limited sources

- Some ScienceDirect full texts were paywalled.
- SSRN PDF delivery endpoints for Huang et al. and Kretschmer et al. returned access restrictions in the web tool; title pages / abstracts / author pages were used instead.
- No Google Scholar snippets were used as sole evidence for a novelty verdict.
