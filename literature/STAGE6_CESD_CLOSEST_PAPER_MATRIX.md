# Stage 6 C-ESD Closest-Paper Matrix

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: novelty re-kill evidence

## Surviving Stage-4 object being audited

The only candidate main contribution is the FULL-only interaction result:

`Delta_M^(B-T)<0`, `Delta_M^(B-X)<0`, but `Delta_M^(FULL)>0`,

so B-T and B-X each select IS while the FULL game makes the three SUs stable and IS pair-blockable.

The model is frozen. This audit does not change primitives.

## Whole-game reconstruction test

No single audited paper reproduces all economically relevant layers:

1. government standards partition `rho in {IS,SU,SW}`;
2. endogenous continuous depth choice by each formal bloc;
3. partition-generated pairwise standard frictions and network graph;
4. firms observing policy and choosing horizontal product locations;
5. downstream price competition;
6. national welfare `CS_i + domestic worldwide profit`;
7. coalition stability evaluated on regime-specific continuation equilibria;
8. FULL-only sign reversal absent from both policy-only and location-only benchmarks.

Reconstructing the candidate requires at least Ruiz for the policy-to-location timing, Gandal–Shy for the international-government coalition architecture, and a separate network/spatial compatibility literature for the product-market mechanism. This is component/structural overlap, not direct whole-game absorption.

## Search limitation

Absence of a located theorem is not proof of novelty. The strongest threat is that a referee may regard C-ESD as a straightforward synthesis of Ruiz (policy -> product characteristics) and Gandal–Shy (international standards unions). The paper therefore must present the FULL-only sign reversal as the contribution, not the combined setup.
