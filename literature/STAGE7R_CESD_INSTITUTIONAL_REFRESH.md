# Stage 7R — Institutional Interpretation Refresh

Date: 2026-09-04

## Purpose

Refresh the Stage-7 institutional mapping after the Stage-4R action-set clarification. No new institutional fact is claimed and no new model primitive is introduced.

## Stage-4R clarification

The policy variable `s_C` is now interpreted strictly as **additional within-coalition harmonization depth conditional on formal regime membership**.

Therefore:

- non-singleton blocs can choose positive harmonization depth;
- singleton blocs have `s_C=0` by definition;
- `s_C=0` does **not** mean zero formal compatibility or zero network relation;
- the formal partition continues to determine the binary compatibility/network graph, while `s_C` governs additional depth on the standard-friction margin.

This clarification strengthens the institutional interpretation because a singleton has no internal pair over which to deepen coalition harmonization.

## What changes in exposition

The manuscript should no longer describe every standards bloc as choosing a continuous depth. Instead it should say:

> A formal standards coalition with at least two members can choose the depth of additional harmonization within that coalition. Singleton regimes have no internal harmonization margin and therefore have depth zero.

Under `SU_12`, this gives

`tau_12 = t_bar - s_12`,

`tau_13 = tau_23 = t_bar + s_12/2`.

The second relation remains a stylized bloc-boundary normalization, not an empirical legal fact.

## Generality conclusion

The repaired mechanism still requires:

1. a formal compatibility or recognition relation;
2. a coalition-level margin for deepening harmonization;
3. network/access value tied to formal compatibility;
4. a distinct product characteristic on which firms can reposition;
5. decentralized welfare objectives that include domestic producer rents.

The repair narrows and improves the institutional meaning of the policy variable; it does not reduce the theoretical generality previously claimed within these bounds.
