# Stage 4R — C1 Multiplicity Repair

Date: 2026-09-10

Status: **REOPENED STAGE-4 CONSTRUCTION OBJECT — NOT A THEORY FREEZE**

## 1. Reason for reopening

Stage 4A established that the low-redesign-cost calibration `gamma=.03` admits at least two pure-strategy SU location equilibria with opposite member-welfare rankings relative to IS. Therefore the prior selection-free FULL stability claim is invalid at that calibration.

The repair does **not** delete the crossing equilibrium by a selection rule and does **not** add a primitive.

## 2. Frozen economic model

C1 remains unchanged:

`partition rho -> bloc depth s -> product positions x -> Bertrand prices -> welfare -> strict blocking/stability`.

For `i!=j`,

`K_ij=c0+lambda*phi(x_i-x_j)/Tau_ij(rho,s)-v*M_ij(rho,s)`,

with linear realized interoperability `M_ij=s_C/s_bar` inside a multi-country bloc and zero across blocs.

All Stage-3 prohibitions remain binding: no nonlinear realization map, policy cost, home bias, asymmetry, extra market, bargaining rule, no-crossing constraint, or dynamic selection rule.

## 3. Repair logic

The Stage-4A counterexample is generated by a low adjustment-cost region in which both members can profitably coordinate on a large leapfrog/crossing relocation. Repositioning cost curvature `gamma` is already a primitive of the audited model. Stage 4R therefore asks whether a **selection-free high-adjustment-cost region** exists in the unchanged model.

This is a theorem-domain repair, not a model repair.

The construction audit first preserves the low-gamma counterexample. It then searches the full three-dimensional location FOC system from dispersed starts and subjects retained roots to whole-circle unilateral best-response attacks.

At `gamma=.05`, no alternative SU location Nash equilibrium is found on the declared 18-history grid spanning `v in {.07,.11,.13}` and policy histories from zero through full depth. This is construction evidence, not an analytic uniqueness theorem.

For the repaired headline illustration, use the more conservative point

`(v,gamma)=(.11,.10)`,

which is deliberately separated from the observed low-gamma multiplicity frontier.

## 5. Policy construction at the repaired point

The exact IS condition remains unchanged. Because `v=.11>1/18`, IS chooses full depth on the symmetric anchor continuation.

For SU, re-solved downstream location continuations on a 21-point full-domain depth grid give strictly increasing member welfare in `s_12` when `s_3=.25` and strictly increasing outsider welfare in `s_3` when `s_12=.25`; hence the construction candidate remains `(s_12,s_3)=(.25,.25)`.

For SW, unilateral singleton-depth deviations are re-solved with the downstream symmetric location continuation. Own national welfare is strictly increasing on the same 21-point grid, yielding the construction candidate `(s_1,s_2,s_3)=(.25,.25,.25)`.

These policy statements must be independently re-attacked at Stage 4A.

## 6. Repaired threshold result

The fixed-position threshold remains exact:

`v_FIX=1/15 approximately .06666667`.

At `gamma=.10`, the construction solver gives

`v_EXO-HIST approximately .09934003`,

`v_FULL approximately .11964007`.

Hence

`v_FIX < v_EXO-HIST < v_FULL`.

The round point `v=.11` lies strictly between the latter two thresholds. Therefore, under the repaired continuation object:

- `B-FIX`: IS is not pair-blocked by SU;
- `B-EXO-HIST`: IS is not pair-blocked by SU;
- `FULL`: IS is pair-blocked by a bilateral SU, and SU members prefer SU to SW.

The headline result is again a FULL-only coalition implication at construction level.

## 7. What Stage 4R does not claim

Stage 4R does not claim:

- that `gamma=.05` is an analytic uniqueness threshold;
- that no multiplicity exists anywhere above `.05`;
- that the repaired SU equilibrium is globally unique on a continuous rectangle merely because finite multi-start searches found no alternative;
- that closest-anchor, order preservation, dynamic convergence, Pareto dominance, or risk dominance selects the preferred equilibrium;
- that low-gamma multiplicity is irrelevant.

The low-gamma crossing equilibrium remains a permanent regression case and must continue to be reported as part of the model's equilibrium geometry.
