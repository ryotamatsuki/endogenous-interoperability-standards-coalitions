\section{Main Results}\label{sec:results}

The contribution is an interaction result. We therefore compare the full game with two mandatory nested benchmarks that shut down one endogenous margin at a time.

\paragraph{Benchmark B-T.} Additional harmonization depth is endogenous for non-singleton blocs, but product locations are fixed at inherited anchors, $x_i=h_i$.

\paragraph{Benchmark B-X.} Product locations are endogenous, but the additional harmonization depth of every non-singleton bloc is fixed at zero. Singleton blocs have zero depth by definition in all versions of the model.

\paragraph{FULL.} Both non-singleton harmonization depth and product locations are endogenous.

For a symmetric prospective union, define the member gain relative to international standardization as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:delta-member}
\Delta_M^k=W_M^{SU,k}-W^{IS,k},
\qquad k\in\{B\text{-}T,B\text{-}X,FULL\}.
\end{equation}
The main question is whether the sign of \eqref{eq:delta-member} can differ only when both margins are endogenous.

\subsection{Policy-induced re-differentiation}

Under $SU_{12}$, deeper union integration lowers $\tau_{12}$ while increasing $\tau_{13}$ and $\tau_{23}$. Holding product locations fixed, this compresses one source of differentiation between member firms 1 and 2. Because product location remains a separate strategic margin, the member firms can respond by moving apart on the circle. The canonical continuation equilibrium exhibits exactly this response.

At the canonical parameter vector
\begin{equation}\label{eq:witness}
(\bar t,v,\gamma,\bar s)=(1,0.04,0.11,0.25),
\end{equation}
the FULL policy choices are
\[
s_I^*=\bar s,
\qquad
s_{12}^*=\bar s,
\qquad
s_3=0\ \text{under }SU_{12}\text{ by the singleton action-set definition},
\]
and $SW$ has no continuous depth choice because all three blocs are singletons. Under $SU_{12}$, the product-location equilibrium is approximately
\[
x^{SU}=(0.084567,\,0.582100,\,0.833333).
\]
Relative to the inherited locations $(1/6,1/2,5/6)$, the two union members move outward while the outsider remains at its anchor. Thus the standardization union compresses standard-based differentiation inside the bloc but induces additional differentiation on the remaining horizontal product dimension.

\subsection{Interaction-induced coalition reversal}

The two nested benchmarks do not generate the regional-union result. At \eqref{eq:witness},
\[
\Delta_M^{B-T}=-0.010167<0,
\qquad
\Delta_M^{B-X}=-0.000434<0,
\]
so international standardization is stable when only harmonization depth or only product positioning is endogenous. In the full model,
\[
\Delta_M^{FULL}=0.001571>0.
\]
Hence each prospective union pair strictly prefers forming an $SU$ to remaining in $IS$.

\begin{proposition}[Constructive regular-region coalition reversal]\label{prop:reversal}
There exists a nonempty regular parameter region in which
\[
\Delta_M^{B-T}<0,\qquad
\Delta_M^{B-X}<0,\qquad
\Delta_M^{FULL}>0.
\]
In that region, the nested benchmarks select international standardization, whereas the full game makes the three two-country standardization unions stable and renders international standardization pair-blockable.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Status of Proposition \ref{prop:reversal}.}
The proposition is constructive rather than a global closed-form classification. At the parameter vector in \eqref{eq:witness}, the repaired v2 action set satisfies the regularity conditions used in the computation. In particular, for every feasible $IS$ and $SU$ harmonization depth at the canonical primitives, the verification searches the relevant cyclic-order and circular-anchor branches and checks whole-circle unilateral location deviations after re-solving the price subgame; the resulting policy choices are then obtained by global scalar optimization over the repaired action sets. The three inequalities above are strict. Conditional on persistence of the selected regular continuation branch, continuity therefore implies a nonempty local neighborhood with the same sign pattern. We do not claim a global parameter-space theorem, nor do we claim that every local location stationary point is globally stable.

The coalition-stability logic at the witness is transparent. In B-T and B-X, an $SU$ member is worse off than under $IS$, so no pair blocks international standardization. In FULL, $W_M^{SU}>W^{IS}$ while the outsider remains worse off than under $IS$ and members also prefer $SU$ to $SW$. Thus an $SU$ is not unanimously blocked by $IS$ and its members do not wish to dissolve it into $SW$. By symmetry, all three two-country unions are stable.

The result isolates the strategic complementarity between the two endogenous margins. Endogenous harmonization depth alone does not overturn the international-standardization outcome, and endogenous location alone does not overturn it. The reversal appears only because policy-induced compression of standard differentiation changes firms' incentives to re-differentiate on the product dimension.
