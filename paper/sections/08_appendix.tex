\section{Proofs and Verification Details}\label{app:proofs}

\subsection{Demand and price characterization}

For an arc $ij$, the indifferent consumer in \eqref{eq:indifferent} allocates the arc between the two adjacent firms. Relative to the midpoint allocation, the boundary shift equals
\[
\frac{p_j-p_i+v(n_i-n_j)}{2\tau_{ij}}.
\]
Summing the two boundary shifts relevant to each firm yields the weighted-Laplacian expression
\[
q=b-\frac12Lp+\frac v2LG_\rho q,
\]
which is \eqref{eq:affine-demand}. If $A_\rho=I-(v/2)LG_\rho$ is nonsingular, rearrangement yields \eqref{eq:demand-reduced}.

For zero marginal production cost, firm $i$ maximizes $p_iq_i$. Since $\partial q_i/\partial p_i=D_{ii}$, the FOC is $q_i+D_{ii}p_i=0$. Under $D_{ii}<0$, the own-price second derivative is $2D_{ii}<0$. Defining $M=\operatorname{diag}(-1/D_{ii})$ gives $p=Mq$. Substitution into demand gives $(I-DM)q=A_\rho^{-1}b$. Nonsingularity of $I-DM$ yields the unique regular interior solution in \eqref{eq:price-system}. This proves Proposition \ref{prop:price} on the stated domain.

\subsection{Conditional location system}

For the cyclic order $1\to2\to3\to1$, geometric Voronoi shares are affine in $x$, which gives \eqref{eq:b-linear}. Because $K$ is fixed conditional on $(\rho,s)$, $q=K(b_0+Bx)=c+Rx$. The price FOC implies $p_i=M_{ii}q_i$, so operating profit is $M_{ii}q_i^2$. Adding the redesign cost gives \eqref{eq:location-profit}. Differentiating with respect to $x_i$ gives \eqref{eq:location-foc}, and differentiating again gives \eqref{eq:location-soc}. The collection of FOCs is linear in $x$. This proves Proposition \ref{prop:location} as a conditional fixed-order result.

The conditional proof does not establish a global location Nash equilibrium on the circle. The computations underlying Proposition \ref{prop:reversal} therefore evaluate each firm's profit after unilateral relocation across the entire circle, recomputing geometric order, demand, and the price equilibrium for every candidate deviation. The v2 repair additionally requires this downstream whole-circle equilibrium to exist at every feasible policy depth used in the policy-stage optimization. At the canonical primitives, the Stage-4R verification checks all feasible $IS$ and $SU$ depths, enumerates cyclic-order and circular-anchor branches on a dense policy grid, applies continuous whole-circle deviation searches, and performs global scalar policy optimization. $SW$ contains only singleton blocs and therefore has no continuous harmonization-depth action. This full-policy-domain continuation statement is computational at the canonical primitives rather than a global analytic theorem.

\subsection{Welfare identities}

Integrating gross network value, price payments, and linear adaptation losses over all arcs yields
\[
CS=A+vq'G_\rho q-\sum_i p_iq_i-TC.
\]
Country $i$ receives one third of aggregate consumer surplus and owns firm $i$, so $W_i=CS/3+\Pi_i$. Taking the difference between $SU$ and $IS$ for a member yields
\[
\Delta_M=\Delta\Pi_M+\frac{\Delta CS}{3},
\]
which immediately gives Proposition \ref{prop:member-threshold}.

For world welfare,
\begin{align*}
GW
&=CS+\sum_i\Pi_i\\
&=A+vq'G_\rho q-\sum_i p_iq_i-TC
  +\sum_i(p_iq_i-C_i^D)\\
&=A+vq'G_\rho q-TC-\sum_iC_i^D.
\end{align*}
Thus price payments cancel exactly as transfers. The ranking in Proposition \ref{prop:global-wedge} is obtained by evaluating this identity at the canonical verified continuation equilibria. Numerical world-welfare levels reported in the text are expressed net of the common baseline $A$.

\subsection{Constructive regular-region verification}

The main reversal uses the canonical parameter vector
\[
(\bar t,v,\gamma,\bar s)=(1,0.04,0.11,0.25).
\]
Under the repaired v2 action set, the grand coalition chooses $s_I\in[0,\bar s]$, the member pair in an $SU$ chooses its single depth $s_{ij}\in[0,\bar s]$, and singleton blocs have zero depth by definition. The Stage-4R verification solves the repaired policy problem using only downstream continuations that pass the whole-circle location-Nash check. At the canonical vector it obtains
\[
\Delta_M^{B-T}=-0.010167,
\qquad
\Delta_M^{B-X0}=-0.000434,
\qquad
\Delta_M^{FULL}=0.001571.
\]
All inequalities are strict. Conditional on persistence of the selected regular continuation branch, continuity implies that the same sign pattern holds on some neighborhood until a regularity boundary is reached. The pre-specified Stage-11R2 audit then evaluates nine nearby parameter vectors spanning one-at-a-time and joint changes in $v$, $\gamma$, and $\bar s$. All nine points yield valid continuations and all nine preserve $\Delta_M^{B-T}<0<\Delta_M^{FULL}$. This establishes a verified local robustness neighborhood in the tested directions; it is not a global analytic parameter classification.
