## 4. Exact IS policy-depth result

At the symmetric anchor configuration, `phi=1/4` for every pair under IS. Define

`c(s)=c0 + lambda/[4(t_bar-s)] - v`.

The symmetric Bertrand equilibrium is

`p=a(b-c)/(2b)`,

`q=a(b+c)/[2b(b+2c)]`.

Per-country welfare is

`W_IS(c)=a^2(3b^2+2bc-c^2)/[8b^2(b+2c)]`.

The Stage-1 symbolic reconstruction obtains

`dW_IS/dc = -a^2(2b^2+bc+c^2)/[4b^2(b+2c)^2] < 0`,

and

`dc/ds=lambda/[4(t_bar-s)^2] > 0`.

Hence

`dW_IS/ds<0`

on the positive regular domain, conditional on the symmetric-anchor continuation. The source witness therefore implies `s_I*=0` for structural reasons. A whole-circle global location-BR diagnostic at eleven policy nodes from `0` through `s_bar` finds no profitable deviation from the anchors.

Classification: `CORRECT`, with the all-policy-node global-location statement remaining numerical rather than analytic.

## 7. Parameter-interpretation audit

The most important economic weakness is the interpretation of continuous standards depth.

The source policy note describes higher depth as greater coherence/specificity and greater internal interoperability. Yet in PR #65 the compatibility graph `G(rho)` and its network benefit are already fixed by coalition membership and do not vary with depth. Under IS, raising depth therefore creates no separate direct interoperability/network benefit; it only moves `Tau` and hence competitive substitutability.

This is mathematically coherent but economically restrictive. It mechanically creates a strong policy-adjustment channel and is the structural reason the IS bloc can select zero depth.

Classification: `CORRECT BUT ECONOMICALLY AD HOC` as a representation of standards depth.

This finding does **not** authorize adding a depth-dependent network benefit at Stage 1.

## 8. Welfare/comparability audit

The equal consumer-surplus split can be defended only by explicitly retaining three equal national consumer populations facing the same aggregate differentiated-product environment. Without that interpretation, `CS/3` is an attribution convention rather than a derived national welfare object.

Foreign firm profits are excluded and domestic firm worldwide profit is assigned to the firm's home country. This ownership convention is internally consistent once stated.

No direct policy cost exists in the source model. Boundary policy choices are therefore admissible and must not be treated as pathologies.

## 9. Stability-rule audit

The stale manuscript defines strict-blocking, exclusive-membership comparisons and explicitly states that:

- IS can be blocked by an SU-forming pair only if both prospective members strictly gain;

However, the source set does not contain one fully explicit post-affine transition/consent correspondence for every ordered pair among `IS`, all SUs, and `SW`. The complete stable-partition operator is therefore `AMBIGUOUS` at Stage 1. The current IS-vs-SU absorption comparison is still well-defined.
