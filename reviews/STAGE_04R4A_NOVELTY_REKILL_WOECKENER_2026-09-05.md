# Stage 4R4A — Closest-Paper Novelty Re-Kill Addendum

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: **BINDING ADDENDUM TO THE STAGE 4R4A REVIEW**

## 1. Reason for addendum

A broader hostile search performed before Stage 4R4A closure found a materially closer paper than the initial shortlist:

- Bernd Woeckener (1999), **“Network effects, compatibility decisions, and horizontal product differentiation,”** Tübinger Diskussionsbeiträge No. 152.

The abstract states that the paper analyzes compatibility and product-design decisions of two competing hardware suppliers with network effects; firms either establish compatibility and differentiate strongly or remain incompatible and locate at the center; and, critically, **a commitment to compatibility becomes more attractive when it is made before product designs are fixed because firms can then soften competition through their location choices.**

This finding materially narrows the surviving Stage 4R4A contribution.

## 2. What Woeckener absorbs

The following claim is **not novel** and is prohibited:

> standards/compatibility chosen first induces firms to differentiate/reposition later in order to soften downstream competition.

That strategic timing and feedback are already present in Woeckener’s hardware model.

The Stage 4R4A fact that `SU_12` causes the two member firms to move away from each other is therefore a useful internal equilibrium result, but **not by itself a publication-level novelty result**.

Likewise, a statement that endogenous differentiation can change firms’ preference for compatibility is too close to Woeckener and must not be used as the headline contribution.

## 3. Additional coalition/differentiation threat

A second relevant family also tightens the novelty margin:

- Gabszewicz, Marini and Tarola, **“Alliance Formation in a Vertically Differentiated Market”** (working-paper version 2015), study a three-stage game in which firms first form alliances, then choose product qualities, then choose prices, and analyze how coalition structures affect equilibrium qualities, prices, and profits.

This is not a standards/network-compatibility model, but it absorbs the generic claim that “coalition structure chosen first changes later endogenous product differentiation and prices.”

Therefore the paper cannot rely on coalition-first timing plus endogenous differentiation as novelty either.

## 4. What may still survive

The remaining candidate contribution is narrower and standards-specific:

1. firms/countries form **technical standards coalitions** whose membership changes both network compatibility and pairwise competitive substitutability;
2. after the standards architecture is fixed, firms incur product-repositioning costs and endogenously alter a **separate product-characteristic/substitutability network**;
3. this post-standard adaptation changes national welfare and the incentives to block or join standards coalitions;
4. as a result, the **stable standards coalition structure or its threshold** differs from the stable structure when product positions are fixed.

This joint result is not supplied by any one of the identified closest papers:

- Woeckener: compatibility -> later product location, but two firms and no multilateral standards-coalition stability;
- Economides–Skrzypacz: standards-coalition formation and network effects, but no costly post-coalition product repositioning;
- Gabszewicz–Marini–Tarola: coalition -> quality -> prices, but not technical compatibility/network standards;
- Baake–Boom: endogenous quality and compatibility, but quality is chosen before compatibility and there is no standards-coalition structure;
- Barrett–Yang: international standard compliance, network effects, and redesign costs, but not a multilateral coalition-stability game with post-standard horizontal repositioning;
- Ushchev–Zenou: product-substitutability network and Bertrand prices, but the network is not endogenously reshaped through standards-coalition-induced repositioning.

## 5. Revised Stage 4R4A novelty verdict

The original Stage 4R4A novelty classification `DISTINCT BUT NARROW` remains directionally correct, but the minimal Stage 4 reversal **does not yet establish enough distance** because its core repositioning mechanism is close to Woeckener.

The binding verdict is therefore revised to:

**CONDITIONAL GO — CONTINUATION AND REPOSITIONING PASS; PUBLICATION-LEVEL NOVELTY DEPENDS ENTIRELY ON THE STANDARDS-COALITION STABILITY RESULT AT STAGE 5R4.**

This is one unresolved architecture/result choice, not authorization for another model repair.

## 6. Stage 5R4 fatal novelty test

Stage 5R4 must compare the endogenous-position model with an otherwise identical fixed-position benchmark and establish at least one of the following on a nondegenerate parameter region:

- a different stable standards-coalition partition;
- a strict shift in the network-effect / repositioning-cost threshold at which a bilateral coalition becomes stable;
- a coalition-blocking reversal that is impossible under fixed positions;
- a private-vs-social coalition-stability wedge generated specifically by post-standard repositioning.

The result must be stated at the **coalition structure / stability level**, not merely as a profit or welfare difference between `IS` and `SU`.

If Stage 5R4 only reproduces “compatibility induces more differentiation and can become more attractive,” return:

**NO-GO — TERMINATE THIS PAPER.**

No further continuation architecture search is authorized after that failure.
